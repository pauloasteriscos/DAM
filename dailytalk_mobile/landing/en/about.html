ABOUT DAILYTALK.PT

A simple idea: arrive better prepared to speak with confidence.

DailyTalk.pt was created to help young people in Erasmus+ school mobility feel better prepared for the small conversations that make a big difference when arriving in another country.

Try now Create account

Why DailyTalk.pt exists

Knowing words in class is different from using them when someone speaks to you, when you need help or when you are meeting a host family. DailyTalk.pt was created to practise exactly those moments.

Made for school mobility

The focus is on real situations for students roughly 11 to 15 years old: arrival, school, meals, transport, routines, culture, living together and growing independence.

An academic project that became a product

DailyTalk.pt evolved throughout the Master's in Informatics Engineering and Web Technologies (MEIW), jointly associated with Universidade Aberta (UAb) and Universidade de Trás-os-Montes e Alto Douro (UTAD). Serious games, Web, mobile, HCI, UX, architecture, security and cloud were applied to the same real problem.

Technology with a purpose

Technology is there to make the experience fast, secure, usable with limited internet and able to keep the student's progress without getting in the way.

DailyTalk.pt grew from a concrete communication difficulty in Erasmus+ school mobility and evolved through the Master's in Informatics Engineering and Web Technologies.
