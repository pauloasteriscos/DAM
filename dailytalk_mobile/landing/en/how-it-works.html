HOW IT WORKS

Learn what you will actually use.

DailyTalk.pt helps you prepare for conversations that can really happen during an Erasmus+ school mobility: from your first hello to asking for help, speaking with classmates or understanding your host family's routine.

Try now Create account

Start with situations you may actually live

Instead of isolated word lists, you find missions linked to arrival, school, meals, transport, host family, social life and everyday moments.

Practise in the way that suits you

Use vocabulary, dialogues, speaking, quizzes and review. You can focus more on words one day and conversation the next without losing the rest of the path.

Keep going even when the internet is not perfect

Travel does not always come with reliable Wi-Fi. DailyTalk.pt is designed to keep working with limited connectivity and sync again when possible.

Your progress follows you

On phone, tablet or computer, the goal is simple: continue where you left off without having to think about the technology behind it.
