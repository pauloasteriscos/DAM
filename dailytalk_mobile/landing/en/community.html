COMMUNITY · EVOLVING

The current core focuses on the learning path, offline operation, progression and synchronisation. Open community, voting, reputation and certification belong to later stages.

Activity creation

The future vision allows users to structure their own activities for situations they encountered or want to prepare for.

Moderation and curation

Public content should pass validation and moderation, with its origin clearly identified.

Votes, reputation and certification

Popularity does not replace pedagogical validation. High-quality content may later be reviewed and promoted editorially.
