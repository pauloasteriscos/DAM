ERASMUS+ SCHOOL MOBILITY

Preparation for the moments when you need to speak.

The real problem

A young person may know vocabulary and rules and still struggle when introducing themselves, understanding a host family's routine, asking for help or responding at a partner school.

Moments in the journey

Before the trip, arrival, host family, first day at school, meals and routines, social life, asking for help, culture and preparing to return.

20/80 principle

The project prioritises highly useful vocabulary, expressions and situations. Its value lies in specialisation in Erasmus+ school mobility, not in a generic language catalogue.
